How can we help you?

Find simple instructions for using your Bank of Airlines account.

01

How do I make a deposit?

Go to Transactions, select Deposit, enter the amount, and submit the transaction.

Go to Transactions →
02

How do I withdraw money?

Go to Transactions, select Withdraw, enter the amount, and submit the transaction.

Go to Transactions →
03

How do I make a transfer?

Go to Transactions, select Transfer, enter the required information, and submit the transfer.

Go to Transactions →
04

How do I check my balance?

Your current account balance can be found on the Dashboard.

Go to Dashboard →
05

How do I check my transactions?

Go to the Dashboard to view your recent transactions. Select View all transactions to see more.

Go to Dashboard →
06

How do I sort my transactions?

On the Dashboard, open the Sort By menu and choose Recent, Oldest, Highest Amount, or Lowest Amount.

Go to Dashboard →
07

How do I view my account details?

On the Dashboard, select Account Details underneath your account balance.

Go to Dashboard →
08

How do I view my transaction activity?

Scroll to the Transaction Activity section on the Dashboard and choose a time period from the dropdown.

Go to Dashboard →
09

How do I log out?

Select your profile in the top-right corner of the Dashboard and select Logout.

Go to Dashboard →
10

How do I access my account after registering?

After creating your account, you will be returned to the Login page. Enter your email or User ID and password to access your Dashboard.

Go to Login →
